Surface Mounted Power Bank

A compact wall-mount power module engineered for stable delivery, safer usage, and practical everyday maintenance.

  • Power Electronics
  • Microcontroller
  • Hardware Design

Problem

Portable power units often compromise either compactness or safety, leading to unstable output and reduced long-term reliability.

Engineering Approach

Combined power conversion design, microcontroller-based monitoring, and layered protection logic to keep output performance controlled under load.

Compact form was important, but safety and delivery stability were the non-negotiables.

Outcome

Built a compact power unit with stable delivery and integrated protection mechanisms suitable for daily operation.
