Project By Anand P S | March 2026 | 6 min read

DC Machines Protection System

A machine protection system that monitors abnormal operating conditions and triggers fast defensive responses.

  • Protection Logic
  • Sensors
  • Firmware

Problem

Fault conditions in DC machines can escalate quickly when detection and shutdown logic are delayed or inconsistent.

Engineering Approach

Implemented sensor-backed threshold detection, structured protection-state handling, and firmware-triggered response flow for rapid fault handling.

Protection logic is only useful when the response is faster than the fault can escalate.

Outcome

Enabled faster fault isolation and automatic protection actions, improving operational safety and resilience.
