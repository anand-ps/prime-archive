Personal Finance Management System

A backend-first finance tracking platform that consolidates income, expenses, and account balances into consistent monthly reporting views.

  • Full-Stack Application
  • Backend Engineering
  • Data Modeling
  • Reporting

Problem

Financial activity was spread across multiple accounts, which made it difficult to monitor cash flow and plan spending from a single reliable view.

Engineering Approach

Designed category-oriented transaction modeling, built reporting aggregations for periodic insights, and created account snapshot views for quick status checks.

The real value came from translating spending noise into decisions that were easy to trust.

Outcome

Delivered clearer cash-flow visibility through categorized expense summaries and dependable account-level reporting.
